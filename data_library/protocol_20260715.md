## QMF operating protocol (IS campaigns, once the filter is installed)

Purpose: RATE equalization + purification. The filter cannot change the
optical lineshapes — the 32/34S asymmetry is imprinted downstream in the
CEC, and the RFQ's buffer-gas cooling erases any energy sculpting the
QMF applies. Use it to control rates; the physics rules below come from
the Extrel peak-shape notes (Pedder RA_2010A/RA_2011A, in Learning zone).

### Setpoint discipline
- Static setpoints only. Per-isotope setpoints by EXACT mass scaling at
  the fixed 2.2 MHz drive: (Vrf, Vdc)_A = (A/32) x (Vrf, Vdc)_32.
  Same relative point in every isotope's stability triangle = no
  differential transmission systematic between reference and signal
  (the QMF analogue of "gates held fixed within a campaign").
- 36S design setpoint: (202, 25) V, ~240 RF cycles at 0.737 eV injection.
- NEVER touch the resolution controls mid-campaign: a resolution change
  shifts the mass calibration (leading edge moves ~3x faster than
  trailing), forcing a full recalibration.

### 32S reference throttling (dead-time rule)
- Target <~1 ion/bunch in-gate for the 32S reference (dead-time pull
  <~0.3 MHz; scans at >~1.5 ions/bunch showed few-MHz pulls).
- Throttle by RAISING Vdc toward the stability apex at fixed Vrf:
  transmission falls smoothly with the setpoint still centred.
- Do NOT throttle by parking on the low-mass flank: that is the fuzzy
  beta->0 (low-frequency) boundary where transmission is quasi-stable
  and drifts with RF amplitude and source potential.
- Matched 32/34 rates also make any rate-dependent part of the CEC
  lineshape asymmetry common-mode — it cancels in the isotope shift.

### Pole bias / injection energy
- Set the injection energy with pole bias alone (controller: +/-200 V;
  effective source potential ~185 V from the col/anticol calibration).
  Fix the campaign value and log it every block.
- Keep the energy LOW: baseline rejection (abundance sensitivity)
  collapses with energy even when the FWHM resolution looks fine.

### One-time commissioning checks
1. QMF on/off A-B at matched rates: fitted 32S centroid AND skew
   parameter eta unchanged within errors => no coupling into the IS.
2. Rate stability >=1 h at the throttled 32S setpoint (drift test).
3. Transmission vs pole bias scan: record any noding / peak-splitting
   structure (RFQ entrance cone acts as a restricted exit aperture).
4. Pole-bias retardation-cliff scan => measured source potential
   (replaces the inferred ~185 V; closes the thesis Ch. 3 todo).

### Every block (add to run sheet)
- QMF (Vrf, Vdc) per isotope, pole bias, and transmitted rate
  (ions/bunch in-gate), logged alongside the He pressure/flow entries.
