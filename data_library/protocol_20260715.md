## QMF operating protocol (IS campaigns, once the filter is installed)

Purpose: RATE equalization + purification. The filter cannot change the
optical lineshapes — the 32/34S asymmetry is imprinted downstream in the
CEC, and the RFQ's buffer-gas cooling erases any energy sculpting the
QMF applies. Use it to control rates; the physics rules below come from
the Extrel peak-shape notes (Pedder RA_2010A/RA_2011A, in Learning zone).

### Setpoint discipline
- Static setpoints only. Per-isotope setpoints by EXACT mass scaling at
  the fixed 2.2 MHz drive: (Vrf, Vdc)_A = (A/32) x (Vrf, Vdc)_32.
  Same relative point in every isotope's stability triangle = no
  differential transmission systematic between reference and signal
  (the QMF analogue of "gates held fixed within a campaign").
- 36S design setpoint: (207, 25) V at 2.2 MHz (REVISED 2026-09-24,
  COMSOL cross-check with the exact round-rod field). The real-rod clean
  36S window at Vdc = 25 V is Vrf = 202.5-215 V, the same at 0.74 eV and
  at 36 eV, so the old (202, 25) V sits on the 34S edge (34S still 90 %
  through at 200 V). SIMION's 0.5 mm grid overstated the field by 2.6 %
  (aligned rods) / 8.3 % (box rods), so the earlier "box rods put the
  window ~3 % lower" and the (195, 22) V suggestion were grid artifacts
  ((195, 22) passes 30 % 34S on the real rods). At 36 eV with the nominal
  2.5 mm beam: 36S T ~ 0.27 at (207, 25), 0.3-0.4 at (202, 25); 32S/34S
  0/150. Still SCAN Vrf on the device (field radius and supply
  calibration are known only at the percent level).
- NEVER touch the resolution controls mid-campaign: a resolution change
  shifts the mass calibration (leading edge moves ~3x faster than
  trailing), forcing a full recalibration.

### 32S reference throttling (dead-time rule)
- Target <~1 ion/bunch in-gate for the 32S reference (dead-time pull
  <~0.3 MHz; scans at >~1.5 ions/bunch showed few-MHz pulls).
- Throttle by RAISING Vdc toward the stability apex at fixed Vrf:
  transmission falls smoothly with the setpoint still centred.
- Do NOT throttle by parking on the low-mass flank: that is the fuzzy
  beta->0 (low-frequency) boundary where transmission is quasi-stable
  and drifts with RF amplitude and source potential.
- Matched 32/34 rates also make any rate-dependent part of the CEC
  lineshape asymmetry common-mode — it cancels in the isotope shift.

### Pole bias / injection energy (REVISED 2026-09-11 entrance-optics sim)
- Target 20-36 eV axial energy inside the rods. For the 136 V source
  potential of the bender beam that is pole bias 116 V (20 eV) to
  100 V (36 eV); recommended start 100 V (controller range +/-200 V).
  Fix the campaign value and log it every block.
- Do NOT run slow: at <~1 eV a quarter to a third of the ions reflect on
  the RF entrance fringe (RF-only T ~ 0.25); at 20-50 eV RF-only
  T ~ 0.5-0.65 (SIMION 0.49-0.57, COMSOL 0.54-0.65) with 32S/34S still
  fully rejected and ~35 RF cycles (R ~ 50-160 vs 18-100 needed).
- Do NOT float higher than ~50 eV either: baseline rejection (abundance
  sensitivity) degrades with energy even when the FWHM looks fine.
- Source potential: ASSUME 136 V for now (decision 2026-09-15); the
  ~185 V col/anticol offset is unreconciled with it -> the
  retardation-cliff scan (check 4 below) settles it. Drive frequency is
  definitively 2.2 MHz (RF head rated 30 pF / 2.2 MHz); all 2.4 MHz-frame
  model voltages scale by 0.84.

### Source / bender tune into the QMF (COMSOL-optimized 2026-09-25)
- Live tune (2026-09-24) in the chain model: the bender loses 55 % and
  delivers a ~20 mm-rms beam 11.5 mm off axis, so ~2 % gets through the
  8 mm QMF aperture, 0.18 % of the 36S through the QMF, none into the RFQ.
- Model-optimized, to try on FCQ (hardware polarity; SUGUI takes
  magnitudes on the negative module): QBBackLeft -307, QBFront -446,
  QBFrontLeft +12, QBBackRight +40, BackLens -1277, LeftLens -976,
  ExitCone -1337 V; QMF steerers 25.5 / 24.5 V (difference ~0).
  Model result: 99 % reach the QMF nipple, 29 % through the aperture,
  3.3 % of the 36S through the QMF (~19x). With the lens plate at 140 V
  (Entrance optics below): 40 % through the aperture and ≈ 7 % of the
  36S through the QMF (phase-averaged). About 0.4 % of the 36S produced
  then reach the RFQ with the thesis loading set, and ~0.7 % with the
  RFQ-entrance retune (QMF -> RFQ section; CAD QMF-RFQ spacing).
- Getting there: step ALL knobs together from the live tune (~10 % of
  the way at a time). Transmission rises monotonically along that line,
  and no single knob moved alone gives more than ~4 %. The quadrants are
  the sharp knobs (+/-10 % on QBBackLeft, i.e. +/-30 V, takes 33 % down
  to 2-21 %), so scan QBBackLeft in ~5 V steps and QBBackRight /
  QBFrontLeft / QBFront in 5-10 V steps. The lenses and the steerer
  difference are flat within +/-10 %.
- CONFIRM the ExitCone wiring first. The model puts it on the extraction
  plate 6 mm in front of the target, but the SUGUI config lists it under
  the RFQ. With that plate at -500 V or grounded, the rest of the tune
  still gives 1.6 / 0.8 % of the 36S through the QMF. The plume
  (0.5 mm spot, cos^4, kT = 1 eV) is assumed.
- Remaining limit: the optimized beam still arrives ~6 mm off axis
  horizontally and half of it hits the lens plate, because the entrance
  only steers vertically (a horizontal steerer is a design item).
- Details: Beamline COMSOL/source_bender/README.md; knob list and
  tolerances in RECOMMENDED_VOLTAGES.md.

### Entrance optics (new plate assembly; Tempus optics channels)
- Aperture plate: 0 V (as built).
- Lens plate: +140 V for the bender beam (plateau 125-150 V).
  COMSOL chain, 2026-09-25: going 100 -> 140 V takes the 36S through the
  QMF from 2.7 to ≈ 7 % of the ions produced (2.6x, phase-averaged).
  Above ~150 V it starts to reflect ions and transmission falls
  (1.7 % at 200 V). On the device, walk it up from ~110 V on FCQ until
  the current stops rising, and back off where it drops. It is tied to
  the source potential (140 V is for 136 V). For the small nominal beam
  it was a weak knob (+/-0.05-0.08 in T).
- Vertical steerers: 0 V, trim in 2 V steps, stay within +/-20 V
  (-0.14 mm/V at the aperture, confirmed in COMSOL; +/-10 V halves a
  centred beam).
- QMF shell: tie to pole bias (ground equally fine); 72 V to pole bias
  with the RFQ-entrance retune (hardly matters).
- Voltages are maintained in Beamline Simion/QMF/RECOMMENDED_VOLTAGES.md.

### QMF -> RFQ coupling (KNOWN BOTTLENECK)
- Spacing (beamline CAD, read from the STEP file 2026-10-01): the QMF rod
  ends sit 1.5 mm INSIDE the RFQ entrance cone mouth (case 0.5 mm inside,
  1 mm short of the rods); 36 mm to the 8 mm funnel throat, 40 mm to the
  5 mm cap. The coupling model used to assume a 60 mm drift before the
  cone mouth. CONFIRM the spacing on the hardware when the cross is open.
- STEP-based RFQ entrance model: the 5 mm endcap aperture, not the cone
  mouth, sets acceptance. The QMF exit beam is 12-15 deg rms divergent.
  COMSOL exit beams handed over 8 mm inside the rods with their RF phase,
  CAD spacing, nominal beam (2.5 mm, +/-1.5 deg) with 36S at the
  (207, 25) V setpoint: 1.6/2.5 % of the input beam into the RFQ at 20 eV
  and 1.2/1.9 % at 36 eV (storage/loading set), vs ~41 % without the QMF
  (layout before it was installed): factor ~16-35. The QMF itself passes
  30/25 % of that beam at the setpoint; 5-8 % of its exit beam gets past
  the cap. Loading set (cap 110 V) admits ~1.6x the storage set.
- The spacing matters: past the cap (36 eV, loading set) 7.8 % at the CAD
  spacing, 3.1 % with a 32 mm drift, 2.0-2.5 % at 60-112 mm. With the
  60 mm drift the model gave ~3x less (and the older random-phase replay
  read ~2x high; both superseded).
- Optional RFQ-entrance retune (voltages only; Beamline COMSOL/coupling):
  - Settings: cone 135 V (about 1 V below the beam energy), loading cap
    105 V (keep >= 105 V: lower admits more but traps less; 110 V keeps
    most of the gain), long DC 1 ~90 V, QMF case anywhere from 72 V to the
    pole bias. The float and the rest of the ladder are unchanged.
  - Result (CAD spacing): about 2x past the cap (nominal beam 7.8 -> 15.4 %
    at 36 eV and 8.1 -> 17.0 % at 20 eV; bender beam 5.6 -> 10.8 %), and
    ~1.9x more trapped in the 10 Pa He drag model (2.8 -> 5.2 %). The gain
    is in the throat -> cap transfer.
  - The cone must track the real beam energy (bender beam: 125 V 6.0 %,
    130 V 7.5 %, 135 V 10.8 %). Walk it up toward the retardation cliff
    and stop a few volts short of it.
- Voltages cannot fix the funnel. With every setting tried, 62-73 % of the
  QMF output hits the RFQ entrance cone / funnel before the 8 mm throat.
  Fix = re-acceleration + focusing (design item). With the QMF exit
  already inside the cone mouth, that stage has to fit without adding
  drift (any drift costs most of the coupling), or come with a larger
  RFQ entrance acceptance.
- Upstream transport matters as much. In the source-to-RFQ chain model
  (COMSOL source + bender from the STEP file, assumed plume and wiring),
  12,000 produced ions with the live tune give 7 36S out of the QMF and
  none into the RFQ. With the model-optimized source / bender tune (see
  above) and the lens plate at 140 V, ≈ 7 % of the 36S pass the QMF.
  About 0.4 % of the 36S produced then reach the RFQ (0.7 % with the
  retune; CAD spacing). Optimize the bender tune on FCQ before judging the
  RFQ coupling.
